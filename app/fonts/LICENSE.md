# Fonts

The two fonts the pages carry. Both came from Google Fonts: `scripts/fonts.mjs` fetched them, and `fonts.json`
says where each came from, how big it is and what its hash is. The copyright lines below are the fonts' own, read from
their `name` tables.

Copyright 2026 Google LLC. All Rights Reserved. Version 2.972, as the font records.

Material Symbols are licensed by Google under the Apache License, Version 2.0
(http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). The file holds only the icons the app draws, cut by Google's own Fonts
API (`icon_names`, size 24, regular weight, outlined), with no other change; `fonts.json` lists the names and the exact
request.
